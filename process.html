Process

From raw file to accepted item.

Four steps, one point of contact, and nothing goes to the buyer until a person has signed off on it.

Follow one example batch through the process. The numbers are illustrative.

  1. Send your data as it is

    A system export, line sheet, spreadsheet or feed. No reformatting on your side — see the short checklist below for what helps us start fast.

    240 not checked yet

    One Excel line sheet arrives: 60 styles, 240 SKUs.

  2. Every field is checked against the buyer's rules

    Columns are mapped to the current PIM template and every value is tested: barcodes, required fields, dropdown lists, pricing and images.

    218 ready22 open

    218 SKUs pass or are repaired automatically. 22 SKUs have 31 open questions.

  3. A person reviews every exception

    Anything the software can't prove is correct goes to a reviewer. Questions only you can answer come back to you in one list, not a stream of emails.

    233 ready7 open

    The reviewer settles 26 questions. The last 7 SKUs wait on 5 questions sent to you in one list.

  4. You receive a ready file and a validation report

    We load the items into the buyer's system, or hand you the finished template. Item data can go in first, with photos added when they arrive. See a sample report.

    240 ready

    All 240 SKUs submitted, with a validation report showing every check.

What to send us first

This is all we need to start — everything else happens on our side.

  • Your product data, in whatever format you already have it
  • The buyer or exchange you're setting items up for
  • Roughly how many products are in this batch (a guess is fine)
  • The date the items need to ship or go live
  • Product photos, if you have them ready (they can follow later)

Frequently asked questions

What file formats do you accept?

Whatever you already have — a CSV or Excel export, a line sheet, a JSON feed, or the buyer's own template partially filled in. We map from what you send; you don't reformat first.

How fast is turnaround?

Turnaround depends on file size and how many exceptions come back to you. We scope timing on a short call before starting, and we build to the tightest deadline in the process, including hour-long asset upload windows.

Is our data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data is used only to complete your submissions and deleted on request. We're glad to sign an NDA before you send anything.

Do you only work with AAFES?

AAFES STEP/PIM is the first system we built the rule-set for, but the process — map, check, review, submit — applies to other retail and exchange PIM systems. Ask us about yours.

How do we send product photos?

A link to a shared folder (Dropbox, Google Drive, WeTransfer or similar) works best for a full batch, since photo sets are usually too large for email. For just a few images, attaching them directly is fine. Photos can follow the item data. We check every image against the buyer's requirements (for AAFES, JPEGs of at least 2000 × 2000 pixels) and flag any that fall short before anything is submitted.

What if our data is missing information?

That's expected. Anything the software can't fill in with confidence goes into one exception list with the specific questions, so you're not chasing details across separate emails.

Ready to get started?

Answer a few quick questions and we'll set up a short call — no file needed yet.
