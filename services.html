Services

Everything between your data and an accepted item.

Send your product data as it is. We handle the mapping, the cleanup, the exceptions and the asset prep it takes to get styles live.

01 · Item setup & PIM data entry

Item setup & PIM data entry

Your columns are mapped to the buyer's current template — brick, category and all — and entered directly into the system or handed back as a ready-to-load file. No guessing which fields a category actually requires.

02 · Data cleaning & validation

Data cleaning & validation

Barcodes stripped of spreadsheet damage (the classic .0 ending, lost leading zeros, scientific notation) and check-digit verified. Colors, sizes, materials and units matched to the exact dropdown values the system accepts. Pricing rules enforced — MSRP at or above unit retail, clean numbers throughout.

03 · Exception review

Human-in-the-loop exception review

Anything the software can't prove is correct doesn't get guessed at — it goes to a dedicated reviewer. Questions only you can answer come back to you as one list, not a scattered stream of emails.

04 · Digital asset prep

Digital asset prep

Product photos audited against resolution minimums (2000 × 2000 for AAFES), matched to the correct SKU, and staged for upload before the association window closes — often measured in a single hour.

05 · Backlog & bulk onboarding

Backlog & bulk onboarding

Coming off manual entry, switching contractors, or sitting on a backlog of styles that never got set up? We take the full batch and work it down to a clean, submitted state.

What we check on every file

Barcodes

Check digits, lost leading zeros, and spreadsheet damage such as “.0” endings and scientific notation.

Required fields

Every mandatory attribute for the item's category is present and in the right format.

Dropdown values

Colors, sizes, materials and units matched to the exact values the buyer's system accepts.

Pricing rules

MSRP is at or above unit retail, and prices are clean numbers.

Category attributes

Only attributes that belong to the item's category are sent, so none are rejected.

Product images

Resolution, naming and SKU matching checked before the upload window opens.

How we price

No generic day-rate quote before we've seen your data. Pricing follows the shape of the work, and it's set on a short call once we understand your volume and deadlines.

Pilot pricing

Hourly with a per-file minimum for your first file or two — this is how we both learn the real effort involved.

Per-style pricing

Once we've timed a couple of real files, ongoing work moves to a predictable per-style rate.

Retainer

Add-ons as needed
